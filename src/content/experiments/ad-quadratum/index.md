summary: The drawing at the top of this page follows one rule. A circle holds a square, the square holds a circle, and again.
technologies: [SVG, CSS]
diagram: ad-quadratum
notes:
  - Each step divides the radius by √2, so each circle has half the area of the one before.
  - Grid lines, nodes, the offset circle and the single terracotta segment are all measured from those radii.
  - The squares turn very slowly around one centre, so their corners never leave their circles. Motion pauses off-screen and stops if you prefer reduced motion.
